## What Apex is

Apex is a quantitative research **operating system**, not a trading bot. It runs an unattended, recurring loop that observes public market data across several venues, turns that observation into durable evidence, evaluates candidate opportunities against that evidence, and records — for every one of those evaluations, whether or not it goes anywhere — a permanent, queryable counterfactual decision. It then follows up on what actually happened afterward, purely as evidence, and routes that back to a human for research judgment.

Everything Apex's domain logic produces today is tagged `authority = "NONE"`. Apex does not currently hold, allocate, or move real capital. It does not currently execute a live order anywhere in its own code paths (see [Authority and Safety Model](AUTHORITY_AND_SAFETY_MODEL.md) for the exact code-level evidence behind that claim). What Apex *does* do, continuously and unattended, is build a rigorous, replayable, provenance-tracked record of what the market looked like, what Apex concluded, and — later — what actually happened.

## What problem it solves

Most research/trading projects either (a) skip straight to placing orders on shaky evidence, or (b) do research once, by hand, with no durable record of what was rejected and why. Apex is built around the opposite discipline: **broad, cheap observation; narrow, explicit, separately-governed execution authority.** The system is designed so that a much later decision to grant any real capital authority can be backed by months of accumulated, replayable, honestly-labeled evidence — including evidence about the decisions Apex *didn't* act on — rather than a backtest run once before going live.

## The autonomous operating loop

A systemd **user** timer (`deploy/apex-operating-cycle-worker.timer`) fires every 5 minutes, invoking one bounded `operating-cycle-worker --once` process against a single fixed plan (`apex-operating-cycle-plan-crypto-book-evidence-v1`). That process:

- Takes a PostgreSQL advisory-lock lease so overlapping firings can't race.
- Evaluates a fixed, ordered list of 18 task types (crypto universe refresh, market pulse, Kalshi refresh, shared-opportunity rebuild, total-market-context refresh, cross-venue allocation, portfolio/mandate refresh, counterfactual ledger sync, follow-up refresh, options capture, performance attribution, crypto/Kalshi/strategy PAPER trial execution, and others), each independently `DUE` / `NOT_DUE` / `BLOCKED` / `UNAVAILABLE`.
- Is idempotent per UTC calendar day — repeated polls and restarts replay the same cycle identity rather than duplicating work or provider requests. Some tasks (the three PAPER trial-execution types) additionally report `awaits_intraday_continuation=True` on a successful dispatch, which tells the cycle *not* to write a fresh terminal result on top of an already-successful one — so they genuinely re-evaluate and can act on every 5-minute tick within a day, without a new `OperatingTaskResult` row each time. One consequence worth knowing: a task's `latest_task_status` can stay frozen on an old `FAILED` result for days while the task is actually healthy and trading every cycle — see PAPER Operations & Observability v1 for the exact mechanism and how to tell the difference.
- Runs under a hardcoded authority record: `capital_authority`, `order_authority`, `broker_authority`, and `financial_mutation_capability` are all `NONE`; `live_eligible` is `False`; network behavior is restricted to `DUE_TASKS_ONLY_EXACT_BOUNDED_PUBLIC_GET`.
- Fails closed, visibly, on a missing/unreachable database rather than silently skipping.

**Added by Live Market Observation Foundation v1, a genuinely separate second timer**: `deploy/apex-live-observer-worker.timer` fires every **1 minute**, independently of the loop above, running one bounded `live-observer-worker --once` round against its own plan (`apex-live-observation-plan-crypto-v1`). It captures top-of-book CRYPTO/Coinbase quote evidence only — no Kalshi, no options, no mandate/ranking logic — and is what a PAPER position's point-in-time mark price is read from (bounded by the caller's own `as_of`, never an unbounded "latest" lookup; see [Authority and Safety Model](AUTHORITY_AND_SAFETY_MODEL.md)). Same lease/heartbeat machinery, distinct plan id, so it cannot race the 5-minute loop's own lock.

See the Operator Guide for how to check either loop's health, and [Authority and Safety Model](AUTHORITY_AND_SAFETY_MODEL.md) for exactly what "authority NONE" means in code.

## The research / evidence loop

Apex's research vocabulary is deliberately layered: an **Observation** is a retained fact; an **Experiment** is a falsifiable test against observations; a **Strategy** is a separately-governed operational method; an **Opportunity** is a later, time-bounded proposal derived from a validated strategy or scan. Evidence is classified by how it was obtained (`EvidenceClass`: `FIXTURE`, `GENUINE_PUBLIC`, `HISTORICAL_PUBLIC`, `SANDBOX`, `PAPER`, `LIVE`) so that synthetic/fixture evidence can never silently substitute for genuine market evidence — a real contamination incident of exactly this kind was found and fixed in 2026-08 (one DOGE-USD row, since flagged ineligible; see the internal engineering log (not shipped)). Every retained record is content-hashed and deterministically reproducible: replaying the same evidence and policy always produces the identical ID.

## The counterfactual / outcome loop

This is Apex's most distinctive mechanism, and it is described in full in [Evidence and Feedback Lifecycle](EVIDENCE_AND_FEEDBACK_LIFECYCLE.md). In short: **every** retained eligibility or mandate decision — whether it advanced, was constrained, or was rejected — becomes a permanent `CounterfactualDecision` row. Later, a `DecisionFollowUpObservation` records objective, source-attributed market facts about what actually happened (a price move, a market status/settlement) — never a judgment like "good rejection" or "regret." Those observations roll up into aggregate, cohort-level `CounterfactualOutcomeAggregate` evidence, which becomes a research-agenda item for a human to review. **This is evidence accumulation, not machine learning** — nothing in this pipeline automatically retrains a model, adjusts a ranking weight, or changes a mandate. See the linked document for the exact code-level guarantees.

## Ranking / portfolio role

Eligible opportunities across venues are normalized into `SharedOpportunityProjection` records, gated to `ELIGIBLE` / `REJECTED` / `EXPIRED` / `BLOCKED_MISSING_EVIDENCE`, then scored by a 10-component weighted `prioritize()` function into an `ACTIONABLE` / `RESEARCH_ONLY` tier (a tier is explanatory, never a filter). An `InvestmentMandate` — versioned, defaulting to **zero capital / nothing permitted** — can then evaluate any eligible opportunity against normalized portfolio state, always failing closed on unknown facts, producing `ALLOWED` / `CONSTRAINED` / `REJECTED`. Full detail in [Opportunity Lifecycle](OPPORTUNITY_LIFECYCLE.md).

## Execution authority ladder (summary)

Full detail in [Authority and Safety Model](AUTHORITY_AND_SAFETY_MODEL.md). In brief: research/evidence/ranking/mandate logic is authority-free by construction (every record it produces says `NONE`). A separate, independently-gated subsystem (`live_readiness` / `live_preflight`) governs the path toward `PAPER` and `LIVE` capital authority via an `ExecutionControlState` (`DISABLED` → `PAPER_ONLY` → `LIVE_BLOCKED` → `LIVE_PILOT_ARMED` → `EMERGENCY_STOP`). No live code path today ever sets `LIVE_PILOT_ARMED`; only isolated test fixtures reach it.

## What Apex explicitly does NOT currently do

- **Does not place live orders or move real capital.** No live order-placement function was found anywhere in `src/apex/` (see [Authority and Safety Model](AUTHORITY_AND_SAFETY_MODEL.md) for the exact search performed). The one code path that does submit a real HTTP order request talks to **Alpaca's own paper/sandbox endpoint**, never Alpaca's live host.
- **Does not guarantee profitability, edge, or calibration.** Essentially every research record in the codebase carries an explicit disclaimer to this effect. Most crypto/Kalshi strategy research to date has concluded `REJECTED` or `EXPLORATORY`, not a confirmed edge.
- **Does not autonomously retrain itself or self-modify from outcomes.** The counterfactual/outcome-feedback loop is observational and aggregate-only; it routes to a human research-agenda item, never to an automatic model, ranking, or mandate change.
- **Does not currently hold any user capital.** The default `InvestmentMandate` is zero-capital and permits nothing until an operator explicitly authors one with real values.
- **Is not exhaustively validated.** Coverage varies widely by domain and most findings so far are negative or exploratory results — that is intended behavior of a disciplined research program, not a defect.
- **Does not manage risk on a live account.** A `LiveRiskPolicy` and risk-decision machinery exist and are exercised in tests, but they gate a path (`LIVE_PILOT_ARMED`) that nothing in live operation currently reaches.
- **Autonomous evidence gathering is not autonomous self-modification.** The recurring loop runs itself and accumulates evidence without a human in the loop for each cycle; it cannot change its own code, research methodology, or policy weights. Any such change is still a human-authored commit.

## Precision notes (as requested by this documentation pass)

- **PAPER vs. live execution gating**: Apex has two internal, fully-simulated PAPER ledgers (crypto, Kalshi) that never leave Postgres, plus one path that reaches a real external provider — Alpaca's paper/sandbox trading API. No path reaches a live brokerage or exchange order endpoint.
- **Two materially distinct paths write into the crypto PAPER ledger — added 2026-09-01, do not conflate them.** The original cross-venue path (`CRYPTO_PAPER_TRIAL_EXECUTION`, account `genuine-crypto-paper-account`) authorizes a PAPER trial only from a cross-venue-allocation line over the Shared Opportunity set; against current retained evidence it remains cash-only, zero positions, a known and already-documented gap (see PAPER Operations & Observability v1). A separate, newer path (`STRATEGY_PAPER_TRIAL_EXECUTION`, account `genuine-strategy-crypto-paper-account`) authorizes a PAPER trial directly from a strategy's own evidence-gated promotion against retained candle history — a different upstream decision path, writing into a different account within the *same* `domain/crypto_paper.py` ledger implementation, not a third ledger. As of this writing, only the strategy-driven path has produced a genuine non-zero position (one OPEN BTC-USD position). Kalshi has only the original cross-venue path; there is no strategy-driven Kalshi equivalent yet.
- **Provider write authority**: governed by an explicit `PermissionClass` ladder (`PUBLIC_DATA`, `PRIVATE_VIEW`, `ORDER_PREVIEW`, `TRADE`, `TRANSFER`, `WITHDRAWAL`) — authentication alone is never treated as implying a dangerous permission (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:2007-2009`).
- **No guaranteed profitability**: stated here plainly, because it is easy for a document like this one to accidentally imply otherwise by describing the system's sophistication. Sophistication of the research pipeline is not evidence of edge.
